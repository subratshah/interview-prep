# 🧩 Common Question Families

*Most behavioural prompts fall into a handful of families. Each has a hidden question underneath and a shape the answer should take.*

## 💥 Failure & mistakes
<!-- related: behav-2, behav-3, behav-16, behav-40 -->

### 🎯 Hidden question
- Do you own outcomes, find root cause, and change behaviour?

### 🧱 Shape
- Pick a **real** failure with real cost — not a humble-brag.
- Own it plainly: "I missed it because I…".
- Spend the time on **root cause** and the **systemic fix**.
- End with how you applied the learning since.

> 🔑 Blame-free for others, accountable for yourself.

## ⚔️ Conflict & disagreement
<!-- related: behav-4, behav-5, behav-20, behav-22 -->

### 🎯 Hidden question
- Can you disagree productively and keep the relationship?

### 🧱 Shape

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  U[Understand their view] --> G[Find shared goal] --> E[Bring data / options] --> D[Decide or escalate cleanly] --> C[Commit and repair]
```

- Describe the other side charitably.
- Show you changed your mind on *something*.
- "Disagree and commit" is a valid ending if you then fully committed.

## 🌫️ Ambiguity & pressure
<!-- related: behav-26, behav-11, behav-28 -->

### 🎯 Hidden question
- Can you make progress without a clear spec?

### 🧱 Shape
- How you **reduced** ambiguity: questions, prototypes, stated assumptions.
- What you decided with incomplete info, and how you kept it reversible.
- How you course-corrected when new facts arrived.

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Leadership & influence without authority
<!-- related: behav-21, behav-30, behav-31, behav-35 -->

### 🎯 Hidden question
- Can you move people who don't report to you?

### 🧱 Shape
- Build the case: data, prototype, cost of doing nothing.
- Map stakeholders; win early allies; handle objections one-on-one.
- Make yes easy: pilot, incremental rollout, reversible step.
- Show how it **stuck** (docs, standards, owners), not just that it launched.

## 🔁 Feedback — giving & receiving
<!-- related: behav-9, behav-25, behav-38, behav-24 -->

### 🎯 Hidden question
- Are you coachable, and can you coach?

### 🧱 Shape
- **Receiving**: honest first reaction → what you understood → what you changed → evidence it changed.
- **Giving**: private, specific, behaviour-not-person, with a suggested next step; follow up.
- Include feedback from juniors too — it signals humility.

## 📈 Impact, priorities & users
<!-- related: behav-12, behav-17, behav-19, behav-23 -->

### 🎯 Hidden question
- Do you choose the right work and measure it?

### 🧱 Shape
- State the goal metric up front, the options, why you picked one.
- Show what you said **no** to and how.
- Quantify the result and who it helped.

> 📝 Practice: for each family, write the hidden question on a card with one story that answers it.
