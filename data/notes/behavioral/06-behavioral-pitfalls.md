# ⚠️ Follow-ups & Pitfalls

*Interviewers probe past the prepared story. Expect follow-ups, avoid the classic failure modes, and use the last minutes for good questions.*

## 🔍 Handling follow-ups
<!-- related: behav-16, behav-36, behav-37 -->

### 🎯 Common probes
- "What would you do differently?"
- "Who disagreed, and how did you handle them?"
- "How did you measure that?"
- "What was *your* specific part?"
- "What happened afterwards — is it still in use?"
- "What if you'd had half the time?"

### 🧱 How to answer
- Short and direct — follow-ups are for depth, not a second story.
- If you don't know or don't remember, say so and reason about it.
- Rephrase an unclear question; pausing to think is fine.

> 💡 Pre-write 2–3 likely follow-ups on every story card.

## 🚫 Common pitfalls
<!-- related: behav-2, behav-15 -->

| Pitfall | Better approach |
|---|---|
| Vague | Name the system, the metric, the numbers |
| "We" everywhere | "I identified the bottleneck and led the refactor" |
| No learning | "What I learned… since then I…" |
| Blaming others | "Here's what happened, here's how I responded" |
| Over-rehearsed | Know the flow, adapt the wording |
| Too much context | Brief S/T; spend time on Action + Result |
| Wrong question answered | Adapt the story; don't force-fit a favourite |
| Humble-brag failure | A real mistake with a real cost |
| Weakness that's a strength | Real weakness + concrete mitigation in progress |

## 🚩 Red flags interviewers note
<!-- related: behav-4, behav-5, behav-9 -->

- Defensiveness when probed.
- Never having changed your mind.
- Hero narratives where the team is invisible or incompetent.
- Ethical corner-cutting presented as pragmatism.
- Stories with no stakes or no result.

> 🔑 Credibility beats polish: a modest true story outscores an impressive vague one.

## ❓ Your questions back

- Interviewing is two-way; prepare 2–3 thoughtful questions.
- Good areas: team priorities, how decisions get made, what success looks like in 6 months, feedback and growth culture.
- Avoid questions the job page already answers.

> 📝 Practice: after each mock, note which follow-up tripped you and add an answer to that story card.
